Result in one paragraph
Mexico's working rules for migration officers are almost all legal instruments published in
the Diario Oficial de la Federación (DOF) by the Interior Ministry (SEGOB): above all the
Lineamientos para trámites y procedimientos migratorios (2012, amended to 2019), which are
in practice the INM's procedures manual for every residence, visitor and regularisation
application, and the Lineamientos Generales para la expedición de visas (2014, replaced
2025, amended 2026), plus the circular on migration documents and forms, the rules for
migration stations, the guidelines on migrant protection, delegation agreements and the
visa-requirement agreements. These are marked "procedural act". True guidance addressed to
officers is thin: a handful of INM protocols (immigration checks, mass flows, COVID-19),
COMAR's procedures manual (2018) and operating guide for child applicants (2022), the
2016 protocol for unaccompanied children, the SIPINNA protection route for migrant children,
three circulars instructing INM staff (child migrants 2010, victims of crime 2011) and,
historically, the INM Manual de Trámites Migratorios (2000) and Manual de Criterios y
Trámites Migratorios (2010). The INM's internal procedure manuals (control and verification,
migration stations, regulation) and COMAR's eligibility criteria are not published.

Sources used
- https://portales.segob.gob.mx/es/PoliticaMigratoria/Nnacional - the Unidad de Política
  Migratoria's "Compendio jurídico migratorio" (Marco nacional; reached from
  /es/PoliticaMigratoria/Normateca). Sections 4 (disposiciones complementarias) and 7
  (documentos históricos) are read. Consolidated PDFs kept by the UPM -> upm/ (Lineamientos
  para trámites y procedimientos migratorios, consolidated to DOF 30-12-2019; circular on
  documents and forms to 04-02-2021; stamp guidelines to 28-11-2022; ...). Every DOF note the
  compendium links to is taken from SEGOB's own DOF system -> dof/. Left out as off topic:
  Mexican passports, legalisations, household goods, welfare programmes, councils, staff
  vetting, uniforms, office addresses. The listing is cached in listings/.
- https://sidof.segob.gob.mx (SEGOB's DOF service; robots.txt allows /notas/getDoc/ except
  for a list of withdrawn notes, which the script honours): DOF notes as Word files, and the
  daily indexes used to find instruments the compendium does not link (EXTRA_DOF in the
  script; days 2010-01 to 2026-11 were scanned for migration-related titles).
- https://www.gob.mx/inm and https://www.gob.mx/comar "documentos" (360 and 396 entries
  read): INM protocols and COMAR manuals -> inm/, comar/; SIPINNA route -> otros/.
- web.archive.org: pre-2012 texts of the INM's old website -> historico/; the 2016 UNHCR /
  COMAR / UNICEF initial-assessment protocol for children (acnur.org refuses scripts).
