# Immigration guidance: Mexico

Published guidance that the authorities of Mexico issue to the officials who decide immigration cases, collected in November 2026.

- **Guidance:** 23 files in this folder. `index.tsv` lists each file with its title, date and source address.
- The files are unmodified copies of what the source published. Where a source refused automated access, copies come from a public web archive; the index says which.
- What is missing, and how to obtain it, is described in `NOTES.txt` and `not-obtained.tsv` here, and for all jurisdictions in [MISSING.md](https://github.com/immigration-guidelines/.github/blob/main/MISSING.md), in the repository that also holds the download scripts. Other jurisdictions are listed on the [organisation page](https://github.com/immigration-guidelines).

Mexico's working rules for migration officers are almost all legal instruments in the official gazette, above all the guidelines on migration procedures (2012, amended to 2019) and on visa issue (2014, replaced 2025), marked `procedural act`. True guidance is thin: some protocols of the National Migration Institute, the refugee commission COMAR's procedures manual (2018) and guide for child applicants (2022), the 2016 protocol for unaccompanied children, circulars to staff, and the historical procedures manuals of 2000 and 2010.

The documents remain the work of the authorities that published them.
